## Requirements

### R1. Absolute Rejection of Current Design
- Do NOT copy the current design. Remove all dashboard UI, telemetry, technical cards, glowing boxes, dense grids, and futuristic SaaS styling.
- Do NOT simply restyle components, change colors, or add blur/animations to the existing layout.
- The existing codebase may be reused where technically useful, but the visual language must NOT be preserved.

### R2. Visual Reference Philosophy & Identity
- **Apple:** Study typography, spacing, storytelling, photography, material depth. Do NOT clone.
- **visionOS:** Study translucent materials, depth, soft highlights. Do NOT turn the entire site into glass.
- **Premium Editorial:** Study giant typography, whitespace, asymmetric grids, image-led storytelling.
- **Identity:**  A PREMIUM PERSONAL PRODUCT (minimal, cinematic, editorial, quiet, expensive). NOT a dark futuristic AI portfolio.

### R3. Color & Material System
- **Light Mode:** Background #F5F5F7, Secondary #FFFFFF, Text #1D1D1F, Secondary Text #6E6E73. Extremely subtle neutral gray borders. ONE controlled cool accent (subtle blue or blue-violet). No neon or rainbow.
- **Dark Mode:** Near-black graphite with extremely subtle atmospheric lighting. No pure black. The gradient should almost disappear when looked at directly.
- **Glassmorphism:** Use with extreme restraint (navigation, hero photo frame, interactive controls, selected overlays, modals). NOT on every card/section.
- **Blur & Shadows:** Blur should create atmosphere (background, depth). Shadows must be extremely soft. Visual hierarchy comes from spacing, scale, contrast, opacity, and depth.

### R4. Typography & Navigation
- **Typography:** Typography MUST become the design. Use SF Pro Display (or Geist/Inter/system-ui). Huge editorial headlines. Typography should occupy large portions of the viewport.
- **Navigation:** Minimal floating navigation (e.g., [ NB ] Work About Lab Contact Resume) with translucent material, subtle blur, tiny border, very soft shadow. It should almost disappear when not needed.

### R5. Hero & Content Sections
- **Hero:** Cinematic introduction using Naveen's actual supplied photograph. Visual composition: TYPOGRAPHY + PHOTOGRAPHY + SPACE (NOT cards/metrics). Treat the photo like a premium magazine cover (large crop, soft blur, elegant glass edge, slow parallax).
- **Featured Work:** NO card grids. Use FULL-WIDTH EDITORIAL SECTIONS. Each project must have a completely different composition to create an art-directed experience.
- **Project Visuals:** Create visuals based on actual projects (UI, architecture diagram, terminal, subtle 3D), not generic gradient thumbnails.
- **Case Studies:** Transition into a premium case-study page (Problem, Idea, System, Build, Verification, Lessons, Result).
- **About/Skills/Lab:** WHO I AM / HOW I THINK (editorial narrative). Group skills logically (NO progress bars). Isolate experimental elements in the LAB section.

### R6. Motion, Micro-Interactions & Scroll Experience
- **Motion:** Must feel expensive. Cinematic page reveals, elegant transitions, gentle parallax, magnetic buttons. NO bouncing cards, random particles, or constant movement.
- **Micro-Interactions:** Subtle hover states (tiny movement, soft highlight, slight magnetic attraction).
- **Scroll Experience:** Tell a story in large cinematic chapters (Hero -> Intro -> Work -> About -> Lab -> Contact). Avoid 20 tiny sections.

### R7. Mobile & Performance
