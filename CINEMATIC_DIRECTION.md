# Cinematic Direction: Visual Philosophy & Aesthetic Directives

## 1. Core Vision & Aesthetic Manifesto

The experience is a **continuous sci-fi cinematic master-shot**. It must feel like an ultra-high-budget sci-fi feature film (Blade Runner 2049, Interstellar, Tron: Legacy, Ghost in the Shell) combined with high-precision engineering visualization.

### Prohibited Tropes (Strict Zero Tolerance)
- ❌ NO cheap purple/pink gradient backgrounds.
- ❌ NO generic glassmorphic cards with thick rainbow borders.
- ❌ NO template dashboards, fake gauges, or arbitrary progress bars (e.g. "Python 95%").
- ❌ NO 2D zoom hacks or discrete scene cuts.

### Required Aesthetic Standards
- ✅ **Deep Contrast Void**: Pitch black / dark titanium obsidian (`#030712`, `#08080A`, `#0B0F19`) foundation.
- ✅ **Controlled Optical Accents**: High-purity Luminous Cyan (`#00F0FF`), Monochromatic Silver/Titanium (`#E2E8F0`), Electric Amber (`#F59E0B`), and Subtle Deep Violet (`#8B5CF6`).
- ✅ **Atmospheric Volumetrics**: Realistic depth haze, subtle Rayleigh particle scattering, dynamic lens flare, anamorphic bokeh, and film grain.
- ✅ **Cinematic Typography**: Crisp uppercase trackers, monospace telemetry coordinate overlays, high-end Swiss/editorial typography (Geist / SF Pro Display / Syne).

## 3. Camera Choreography & Pacing Rules

1. **Unbroken Continuity**: The camera never cuts. Position and rotation interpolate smoothly along the 3D Catmull-Rom spline.
2. **Velocity Profiles**:
   - *Scene 01*: Slow, drifting, atmospheric pan.
   - *Scene 02*: Epic crane descent revealing the AI world horizon.
   - *Scene 03*: Dramatic linear acceleration towards the giant humanoid robot's chest/head aperture.
   - *Scene 04*: Microscopic macro dive inside the neural lattice; depth of field tightens.
   - *Scene 05*: High-speed warp tracking along the electric signal pulse.
   - *Scene 06*: Dramatic pull-out and upward elevation revealing the digital city grid.
   - *Scene 07*: Smooth architectural glide through the entrance portico into the pristine portfolio space.
3. **Anamorphic Lens Feel**: 2.39:1 letterbox aesthetic cues, subtle barrel distortion at screen edges, and chromatic aberration expanding under high scroll velocity.
